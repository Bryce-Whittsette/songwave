# SongWave 0.5.6 — Your music only

Public Preview · November 26, 2026

Already on 0.5.4 or later? Choose **SongWave → Check for Updates…** and approve the update. [Download for Mac](https://github.com/Bryce-Whittsette/songwave/releases/download/v0.5.6/SongWave-0.5.6-AppleSilicon.dmg).

## What changed

- Removed Play Demo from the player and Playback menu.
- Removed the built-in demo option from Ultra.
- Deleted the generated demo audio and its playback paths. SongWave follows music playing on your Mac or a local file you choose.
- Ultra retains its stronger temporary processing profile from 0.5.5; your saved EQ stays unchanged.

Ultra stays quiet when no music is playing. Its 45-second timer, cooldown, thermal stops and call suppression remain. This preview is not notarized.
