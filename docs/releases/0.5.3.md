# SongWave 0.5.3 — Bass sustain

Public Preview · November 26, 2026

Low bass now has its own soft rumble underneath the individual drum taps in **Speakers + haptics**.

## Download and install

**[Download SongWave for Mac](https://github.com/Bryce-Whittsette/songwave/releases/download/v0.5.3/SongWave-0.5.3-AppleSilicon.dmg)**

1. Open the downloaded DMG.
2. Drag **SongWave** into **Applications**, then open it from Applications.
3. Finish the short setup and allow **System Audio Recording**. Play music normally in Spotify, Apple Music, or your browser, and rest a finger lightly on the trackpad.

No Terminal commands, song link, or audio file are needed. Links and files are optional ways to try other songs.

**Updating?** Quit SongWave first. Drag the new app into Applications and choose **Replace**. Listening preferences stay saved. macOS may request audio access again because this preview uses ad hoc signing.

Requires **Apple silicon, macOS 14.2 or later, and a Force Touch trackpad**. Tested on an M4 MacBook Air. The app has **not been notarized by Apple**. If macOS blocks it, follow [SongWave's installation help](https://github.com/Bryce-Whittsette/songwave/blob/main/docs/troubleshooting.md#macos-wont-open-the-app).

## What changed

- **Bass sustain:** feel a low-bass rumble alongside the separate percussion taps. In **Fine Tune**, set Bass sustain to **0%** for taps only, or raise it to bring in the rumble.
- **Transient emphasis:** lower settings give drum hits a longer, rounder fade; higher settings give a shorter, crisper tap. It shapes the fade, rather than changing beat-detection sensitivity or timing.
- **More regression coverage:** checks now cover sustained bass, voice-shaped sounds, silence, disabled output, and the two tuning controls independently.
- **Project documentation:** installation help, streaming-engine decisions, and mobile feasibility are documented in this repository.

These changes apply to automatic **Speakers + haptics**. Trackpad only retains its Light and Isolated vocal modes. Custom AHAP patterns keep their authored curves. Bass sustain does not generate extra keyboard-light beats.

## Known limits

Music recognition and vocal separation are approximate; very low voices and dense mixes can still confuse the rhythm detector. Some protected audio cannot be captured. Timing depends on the audio route and can need manual adjustment. Trackpad and keyboard effects use undocumented macOS interfaces. Ultra is limited to 45 seconds with a cooldown; these are software limits, not a manufacturer safety rating.

<summary>Developer notes: signal processing, tests, and measurements</summary>

- The speaker path continues to use per-process Core Audio taps and native DSP. This release adds no neural model, audio rerouting, network request, or runtime dependency to that path.
- `BassEnvelope` uses a causal 25 Hz high-pass stage, two 100 Hz low-pass stages, and an energy follower with 8 ms attack and 35 ms release constants. Existing FFT pitch evidence gates output to 38–85 Hz with at least 16 ms of stable evidence. Sustain is capped at 0.28 before user gain and volume following.
- `Tuning.bassSustain` defaults to 0.6, migrates saved settings, and supports 0–1. `Tuning.impact` now reaches the automatic percussion renderer and changes its decay shape. Sustain remains independent of discrete beat events.
- Regression coverage includes sustained 55/70 Hz signals and rejection of synthetic 95/125/175/225 Hz voiced syllables at 16, 24, 44.1, and 48 kHz; sustain bypass; silence; suppression; independent transient shaping; and the existing simulated live routes. Synthetic fixtures do not establish accuracy on every song.
- On the development M4 MacBook Air, a single optimized DSP-only run processed 60 seconds of precomputed PCM in 0.239 seconds versus 0.229 seconds for 0.5.2. Maximum resident memory was approximately 4.05 MB versus 4.03 MB. These are not whole-app RAM measurements or a speed improvement claim.
- A synthetic 65 Hz onset produced a first percussion event at +16 ms and bass sustain at +32 ms. Capture, output scheduling, and physical motor response are excluded. The added envelope state is fixed-size at 104 bytes; no additional lookahead buffer is introduced.
- Rapidly SDK and HS-TasNet are not bundled. License, checkpoint, native integration, and validation requirements are recorded in [Streaming engine decisions](https://github.com/Bryce-Whittsette/songwave/blob/v0.5.3/docs/streaming-engine.md). No clean drum/bass stem separation is claimed for this DSP path.
