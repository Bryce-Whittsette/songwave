# Short launch recording

**Status: outline ready; recording pending.** Capture the actual app on a working Mac. A screen recording can show interaction and visual feedback; a person's tactile experience requires their own account of it.

Aim for 45–60 seconds. Use the exact downloadable build shown in the caption, audio cleared for public use, and a clean desktop. Keep account names, private playlists, notifications and personal paths out of frame. Review the exported recording before publishing it.

| Time | Show | Caption or spoken point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | Open SongWave; start cleared music in another app. Keep optional Sources collapsed. | “Play music on your Mac. No link needed.” |
| 8–20 s | The main listening view, Beat cue and Music level. | “Follow rhythm through the trackpad and visual feedback.” |
| 20–30 s | Select Beat, Voice and Both; adjust Intensity with two fingers, then click to show the slider alternative. | “Choose what to follow and how strong it feels.” |
| 30–38 s | Turn haptics off while audio and visual feedback continue; turn them back on. | “Keep the music and visual rhythm, with or without vibration.” |
| 38–48 s | Briefly open Tools to show the three categories, then return to listening. | “Optional EQ and experiments stay out of the way.” |
| 48–60 s | Show the repository download link and supported-platform note. | “Experimental Mac app. Current limits and test coverage are in the repository.” |

Ultra is optional for the launch clip. If shown, keep its warning, 45-second limit and cooldown visible, leave keyboard lighting off for the main clip, and label any trimmed footage. Do not speed up a clip to imply tighter sync. Avoid rapid cuts or flashes; provide captions and a readable description of the controls.

Before release, confirm that the recording's build, download link and captions match.
